# After Action Review

A lean, fill-on-screen AAR that prints to Letter or saves as PDF. Usable by any member for any
activity. Tick **For a cadet SDA** to add the achievement field and a completeness self-check.

## Built on

| Source | What it drives |
|---|---|
| CAPP 60-32, *Staff Duty Analysis Guide* (May 2023), §3.7 and rubric A1.7 | Every rubric item has a home on the form; 11-topic content outline (address 5, with 3 subtopics each); 500-700 words |
| Army TC 25-20, *A Leader's Guide to After-Action Reviews* | The four questions: what was supposed to happen, what happened, why, what to sustain and improve |
| FEMA HSEEP After-Action Report / Improvement Plan | Action plan with owner and due date |

## Sections

1. Goals and results: goal, measure, result as data, Met / Partly met / Not met (rubric 2, 7)
2. What happened: factual summary (rubric 3)
3. Sustain: topic, what worked, why (rubric 4)
4. Improve: topic, what fell short, root cause and fix (rubric 5)
5. Action plan: action, owner by duty position, due (rubric 12)
6. Bottom line for next year's team (rubric 6, 12)

Header: activity, dates, location, author grade / last name / CAPID / duty position, unit,
key staff (rubric 1), and who gave input and how (rubric 10).

## Behavior

- Picking a topic in Sustain or Improve shows that topic's CAPP 60-32 subtopics as the prompt.
- Empty rows, unticked boxes, hints and the self-check do not print.
- Nothing is saved (D-004). The page warns before closing with work on it.
- `?sda` in the URL opens it in SDA mode.

The self-check tests completeness only. The senior mentor still scores the rubric.
