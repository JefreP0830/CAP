# After Action Review

Jef's five-part AAR form (first built in Antigravity), rebuilt in the CAP Tools style.
Fill it on screen and print to Letter or save as PDF, or print it blank to fill by hand.

## Parts

1. Administrative Data: event title, dates, location, charter unit, SDA#; author CAPID,
   rank, name, duty position; stakeholders consulted and consultation methods.
2. Execution & Overview: planned objectives; operational reality.
   Continued: mission objectives table (goal, Met / Missed, target metric, actual result).
3. Analysis of Performance: sustains (success factor, root cause); improves (fail point,
   root cause).
4. Recommendations & Conclusion: corrective actions; conclusion and leadership takeaways.
5. Administrative Review & Endorsement: author certification; reviewing officer comments,
   signature and date.

## Behavior

- Load Sample AAR fills a worked example (fictional names, unit and CAPIDs).
- Text boxes grow with their content, on screen and on paper.
- A blank form prints on three fixed pages; a filled form flows onto as many pages as it needs.
- Author name, rank and CAPID in Part I and Part V mirror each other.
- Nothing is saved (D-004). The page warns before closing with work on it.
