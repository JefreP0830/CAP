Part III: Analysis of Performance

Ref: Cause-and-Effect Analysis · CAPP 60-32 Outline Standards

Analysis Principles: Conduct an objective, blameless evaluation focused on systemic processes, resource allocation, communication pathways, and procedural execution across key operational domains (e.g., Goals & Objectives, Human Capital, Program/Curriculum, Schedule & Tempo, Logistics & Resources, Transportation, Communications, Marketing / Public Affairs, Safety & Risk Management, Budget, Administration).

1. What Went Well and Why? (Sustains)Success Factors & Repeatable Processes

Identify specific procedures, preparations, equipment, leadership decisions, or inter-team dynamics that produced success. Explain the cause-and-effect mechanism so it can be institutionalized for future activities.

Success Factor (What worked?)Root Cause (Why did it work?)
2. What Did Not Go as Planned and Why? (Improves)Fail Points & Root Cause Analysis

Analyze friction points, operational delays, resource bottlenecks, or communication breakdowns. Identify the underlying systemic root cause (inadequate planning, unclear expectations, resource deficit, or external dependencies) rather than assessing personal blame.

Fail Point (What went wrong?)Root Cause (Why did the failure occur?)

Part IV: Recommendations & Conclusion

Ref: Actionable Continuity · Service Learning Integration
1. Actionable Corrective Actions (Preventive Solutions)

List actionable recommendations to prevent identified fail points and improve future iterations.

2. Conclusion & Leadership Takeaways

Summarize key takeaways, operational impact, and lessons learned to benefit future leadership teams.
