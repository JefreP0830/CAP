## 2.0.0 - 2026-11-22

- Rebuilt to match Jef's original Antigravity AAR form, part for part and field for field,
  in the CAP Tools style (gray section titles, CAP Ultramarine, Arial body, Rajdhani title,
  no icons). The lean 1.0.0 redesign is retired; it stays in git history.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-22

- Rebuilt from the Antigravity AAR form. Structure now follows the CAPP 60-32 AAR rubric (A1.7)
  and content outline, the TC 25-20 four questions, and the HSEEP improvement plan.
- Removed: Part V certification and reviewing-officer signature (the SDA rubric is the review),
  the separate stakeholder and consultation-method lists (now one tick-box line), duplicate
  narrative boxes, and citations that do not exist (CAPR 60-1 §5.2 AAR content, "AFDP 1-1 4-part
  AAR standard", "USAF Core AAR Standard").
- Added: SDA mode (achievement field, self-check with live word and topic counts), topic picker
  with subtopic prompts, action owner and due date, print that drops empty rows.
- App icon: AAR on CAP Ultramarine.
