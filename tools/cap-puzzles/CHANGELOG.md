# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-22

- Crossword puzzle: topic is the title, CAP category is the byline; 10 x 10 or 15 x 15 grid.
- Answer key on its own page; print puzzle, key, or both on letter paper.
- Built-in word lists for 17 topics across Aerospace Education, Cadet Programs,
  Emergency Services, Communications, Leadership, Safety, and CAP History.
- "Use my own words" for any topic, plus a copyable prompt for building a list with Claude.
- App icon for the CAP Tools menu, favicon and home screen.
