Honor Cadet Evaluator

Five categories, scored 1 to 3, with an observed example behind every score that stands out.

Every two months the unit names one Honor Cadet. Here is how the tool gets you there.

First, score the cadets

Flight commanders and the first sergeant, near the end of each two-month period

  1. Open the Score tab. Type your grade and name, pick your role, and type the period (for example, Jan–Feb 2027).
  2. Tap Add cadet for each cadet you lead. Type their grade and name.
  3. Tap 1, 2 or 3 in each of the five categories. The line under the buttons tells you what that score means. Score only what you saw this period.
  4. Gave a 1 or a 3? Write one thing you saw and when. The red note goes away once you do.
  5. Tap Copy my scores and send the block to the cadet commander by email or text. Do this before you close the page: nothing is saved.

Then, add up the scores

Command staff

  1. Open the Tally tab. Paste each evaluator's block and tap Add to tally.
  2. Check that every evaluator is listed under Evaluators included.
  3. Read the ranking and the observed examples. Ties and incomplete scores are flagged.

Finally, pick and announce

Command staff

  1. Open the Select tab. Choose the cadet. The scores help you decide; they don't decide for you.
  2. Write the reason, using the observed examples, and set the announcement date.
  3. Tap Build announcement. Read it at the last meeting of the period or the first meeting of the next, and post the Honor Wall entry with a photo.

Scoring on paper? Print the blank score sheet from the Rubric and rules tab, then type the scores in later.

Recommended program rules

    
    
      
  • Two-month recognition cycle; a new Honor Cadet every two months.
    • 
      
  • Flight commanders and the first sergeant score; the command staff selects.
    • 
      
  • Announce at the last meeting of the period or the first meeting of the next; a certificate or similar recognition.
    • 
      
  • No uniform accent. The recognition is the reward.
    • 
      
  • Not a duty position. Duty positions stay based on ability, experience, qualifications and unit need.
    • 
      
  • Priority consideration to mentor prospective cadets, if willing; it carries no authority over other cadets.
    • 
      
  • Honor Wall: name, photograph, and the period earned.
    •
