Honor Cadet Evaluator

Five categories, scored 1 to 3, with observations behind every score that stands out.

Every two months the unit names one Honor Cadet. Here is how the tool gets you there.

First, score your cadets

Command team: C/CC, C/DC, C/XO, C/1st SGT, C/Flt CC, C/Flt SGT, near the end of each two-month period

  1. Open the Score tab. Enter your CAPID, last name and role. The recognition period fills itself in; change it only if it's wrong.
  2. Optional: load this week's Cadet Full Track Report. Then typing a CAPID or a last name fills in the other.
  3. For each cadet, enter CAPID, last name, flight and duty position. Tap Add cadet for the next one; the flight carries over.
  4. Tap 1, 2 or 3 in each of the five categories. The line under the buttons tells you what that score means. Score only what you saw this period.
  5. Gave a 3? Write it under Why. Gave a 1? Write it under Why Not. Say what you saw and when.
  6. Tap Submit. The tool checks your sheet, builds a PDF and opens your share menu. Send it to the cadet commander. On a computer the PDF downloads; email it from there.

Then, tally the scores

Command staff

  1. Save every submitted PDF to one folder.
  2. Open the Tally tab and drop all the PDFs on the box, or tap Choose PDFs. The tool reads the scores out of them and ranks the cadets.
  3. Check that every evaluator is listed. Ties and incomplete scores are flagged.

Finally, select and announce

Command staff

  1. Open the Select tab. The top-ranked cadet is picked and the reason is drafted from the Why observations. The scores help you decide; they don't decide for you, so change either one.
  2. Tap Save selection record. Keep the PDF; it is the unit's record of the decision.
  3. Read the announcement at the last meeting of the period or the first meeting of the next, and post the Honor Wall entry with a photo.

Scoring on paper? Print the blank score sheet from the Rubric and rules tab, then enter it here and submit.

Recommended program rules

    
    
      
  • Two-month recognition cycle; a new Honor Cadet every two months.
    • 
      
  • The cadet command team scores; the command staff selects.
    • 
      
  • Announce at the last meeting of the period or the first meeting of the next; a certificate or similar recognition.
    • 
      
  • No uniform accent. The recognition is the reward.
    • 
      
  • Not a duty position. Duty positions stay based on ability, experience, qualifications and unit need.
    • 
      
  • Priority consideration to mentor prospective cadets, if willing; it carries no authority over other cadets.
    • 
      
  • Honor Wall: name, photograph, and the period earned.
    • 
    

    
Each unit commander sets the final rules. Adjust before launch.
