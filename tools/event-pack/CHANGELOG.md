## 2026.11.25

First build, from the Drill Evaluator's layout, tally bar, hotwash and PDF share flow.

### Content (Ground & UDF Team Tasks, 24 May 2004)
- 57 items from Task O-0001 (1-MAR-01): on your person, pack, first aid kit, survival kit, SAR equipment, leader's equipment, with (T) markings kept.
- O-0001 performance measures 3 and 4 graded as "Worn checks": pack secure when worn; knives approved and worn correctly (sheath-knife limits from the training outline).
- Three standards: Trainee minimum (22 items), Ground Team Member (50), Ground Team Leader (57).
- Current-practice notes where the 2004 wording is dated: BDU (now the current field uniform), CAPF 76 card (no longer issued), phone change (cell phone). Notes never change what is required.
- Whistle: not in the national O-0001 list (the guide's lettering skips j). Shown as recommended, not graded.
- Pass rule: every required item present and Ready, no substitutions. "If needed" items (comb, coat, personal medication) can be rated Not needed.

### Workflow
- Member CAPID, inspector CAPID, optional activity name. Nothing stored on the phone.
- Hotwash: refit list built from the ratings (Replenish = missing, Repair = needs refit), plus Sustain and Improve (5 words minimum each).
- PDF: result, required items with ratings, refit list, sustain, improve, inspector line. Share, Save PDF, Share again.
- Refit Guide tab: Task O-0002 4 R's as a tap-when-done checklist.
- Packing List tab: all items with the level that requires them; prints on letter paper.

### Brand
- Section titles and subtitles in gray (Nov 2026 rule); otherwise the Drill Evaluator styles.
- Home-screen icon: a day pack on Ultramarine (generic mark, not a CAP emblem).
