# Event Pack Inspection changelog (tools/event-pack)

## 2026.11.25g

- EVENT is the base standard only (9 items): CAP ID and 101 card, charged cell phone, whistle, day pack, 2 quarts of water, meal and snacks, flashlight, personal first aid kit, personal medication (if needed).
- Removed from Event: change of socks (no clothing spares for a day event; members change at home), rain gear, warm layer, sunscreen and insect repellent, reflective vest (added per event for weather or conditions).
- Spare clothing stays in MULTI-DAY.

## 2026.11.25f

- EVENT ( 8-10 Hrs ) is now a 14-item day-activity safety list, separate from the 24-hour pack: CAP ID and 101 card, charged cell phone, whistle, reflective vest near roads or after dark (N/A otherwise), day pack, 2 quarts of water, meal and snacks, rain gear, warm layer (if needed), change of socks, flashlight, personal first aid kit, personal medication (if needed), sunscreen and insect repellent (if needed).
- Drawn from O-0001 with judgment; labelled on screen and PDF as not a CAP-published standard. 24 HR and MULTI-DAY unchanged (O-0001).
- Left out of Event: matches, light stick, knife and knife check, work gloves, notepad, watch, comb, tissues, pack-secure check.

## 2026.11.25e

- Removed "Field uniform with cap": it is uniform, not pack equipment. O-0001 lists it, so check the uniform in a uniform inspection. Counts now Event 21, 24 HR 49 / 56, Multi-Day 33 / 68 / 75.
- Kept as pack items: spare uniform, spare rank and cutouts, shoe shine kit, sewing kit (carried in the extended pack, not worn); reflective vest, watch, ID (equipment carried on the person).

## 2026.11.25d

- Opens straight to the inspection (Event pack); no standards screen, no "Back to standards".
- Page tabs Inspection, Refit Guide, Pack List moved into the tally bar between the two blue lines; "In progress" status removed.
- Pack type selector below the lower blue line, all caps, black when selected: EVENT ( 8-10 Hrs ) | n ITEMS, 24 HR n ITEMS, MULTI-DAY ( 24-72 Hrs ) | n ITEMS. Item count follows the chosen standard.
- Standard row (gray when selected) under 24 HR (Member, Leader) and MULTI-DAY (Trainee, Member, Leader).
- Items, counts and tally change with the selection; ratings already made carry over.
- Pack List tab uses the same selector and shows only the items required for it; recommended items in one line; prints on letter paper.

## 2026.11.25c

- Pack selector tiles smaller, with durations: Event (8-10 Hrs), 24-Hr, Multi-Day (24-72 Hrs). Explanation line under the selector removed.
- Inspection date picker beside Member and Inspector CAPID (defaults to today; goes on the PDF).
- Rating is now P (pass) or F (fail), on the right of each item's line; "Needs refit" removed (worn-out or used-up items fail). N/A shown only on if-needed items (comb, coat, medication, tent, spare rank, shaving kit).
- Item notes removed. Under an item, only a permitted substitute is shown (e.g. "or penlight"); same in the packing list and PDF. Not-graded summary lines removed from the inspection page.
- Result wording Pass / Fail on screen and PDF; refit list is the F items.

## 2026.11.25b

- Pack selector at the top of the Inspection tab: Event, 24-Hr, Multi-Day.
  - Event: Event minimum (the 24-hour pack items marked T, 22 items).
  - 24-Hr: Ground Team Member (50), Ground Team Leader (57).
  - Multi-Day: Trainee (34), Ground Team Member (69), Ground Team Leader (76); adds the O-0001 extended duration pack ("typically 48 to 72 hours"): backpack, tent (if not shared), 5 meals, sleeping bag and pad, waterproof clothing bag, toilet kit, spares; optional items shown as recommended, not graded; measure 5 (extended pack secure when worn) graded.
- Titles, share text, PDF title and file name carry the pack type (e.g. "Multi-Day pack: Ground Team Member").
- Packing list marks extended-pack items "Multi-Day".

## 2026.11.25

First build, from the Drill Evaluator's layout, tally bar, hotwash and PDF share flow.

### Content (Ground & UDF Team Tasks, 24 May 2004)
- 57 items from Task O-0001 (1-MAR-01): on your person, pack, first aid kit, survival kit, SAR equipment, leader's equipment, with (T) markings kept.
- O-0001 performance measures 3 and 4 graded as "Worn checks": pack secure when worn; knives approved and worn correctly (sheath-knife limits from the training outline).
- Three standards: Trainee minimum (22 items), Ground Team Member (50), Ground Team Leader (57).
- Current-practice notes where the 2004 wording is dated: BDU (now the current field uniform), CAPF 76 card (no longer issued), phone change (cell phone). Notes never change what is required.
- Whistle: not in the national O-0001 list (the guide's lettering skips j). Shown as recommended, not graded.
- Pass rule: every required item present and Ready, no substitutions. "If needed" items (comb, coat, personal medication) can be rated Not needed.

### Workflow
- Member CAPID, inspector CAPID, optional activity name. Nothing stored on the phone.
- Hotwash: refit list built from the ratings (Replenish = missing, Repair = needs refit), plus Sustain and Improve (5 words minimum each).
- PDF: result, required items with ratings, refit list, sustain, improve, inspector line. Share, Save PDF, Share again.
- Refit Guide tab: Task O-0002 4 R's as a tap-when-done checklist.
- Packing List tab: all items with the level that requires them; prints on letter paper.

### Brand
- Section titles and subtitles in gray (Nov 2026 rule); otherwise the Drill Evaluator styles.
- Home-screen icon: a day pack on Ultramarine (generic mark, not a CAP emblem).
