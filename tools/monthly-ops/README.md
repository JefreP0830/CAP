# Monthly Ops Checklist

Recurring compliance tasks for an adult staff, FY Oct–Sep: every meeting
night, by week of the month, by quarter, the once-a-year dates, and the
deadlines that start when something happens.

- Filter by **period** (a month, any month, sources) and **officer** (OPR).
- Print from the browser (Ctrl+P, letter). A full month prints front and
  back; one officer's month fits on one page.
- Red = a date or clock set by a regulation. Gray = squadron target.

Source of truth for currency: titles and effective dates on the national
publications indexes, plus the approved NER and VTWG supplements.

Task data lives in the DATA block at the top of the script in `index.html`.
No member data; nothing is saved in the browser.
